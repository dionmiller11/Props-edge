BETTING TIPS
⚡ SHARP BETTING REMINDERS
🎯Focus on 80%+ hit rates — that's where real edge lives. 70-79% is noise.
🔥Streaks of 5+ games in a row are the strongest signal — momentum is real in props.
📊Cross-reference OVER plays with the opposing pitcher/defense before pulling the trigger.
🎰Max 4-leg parlays. Beyond that, variance kills you even with 80% hit rates.
💡UNDER plays at 80%+ are often more +EV than OVERs — books shade lines toward overs.
⏰Run the scan within 2 hours of first pitch — lineups lock late and pitchers scratch.
